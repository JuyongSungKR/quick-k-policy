Privacy Policy for Quick-K

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Quick-K processes data when integrating with third-party social media APIs, specifically TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Data Collection & Access

Quick-K only accesses account identifiers (such as TikTok open_id and display name) strictly necessary for authenticating the video publishing session. We do NOT collect personal user browsing data, financial data, or sensitive information.

3. Data Usage & Security

OAuth tokens are securely stored in private cloud databases and are used solely to schedule and execute automated video postings requested by the account administrator. Data is never shared or sold to external advertisers.

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

Users may revoke permissions at any time via TikTok App account settings. All associated authorization records are expunged upon revocation.
